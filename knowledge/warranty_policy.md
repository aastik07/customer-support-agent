# Warranty Policy — NovaBuy

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

NovaBuy stands behind the quality of every product we sell. This policy explains
the warranty coverage available on NovaBuy own-brand products and third-party
products sold through our platform.

---

## 1. NovaBuy Own-Brand Warranty

All products manufactured and sold under the NovaBuy brand are covered by a
**2-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery.

### 1.1 What Is Covered

The NovaBuy warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that arise
under normal use, including:

- Manufacturing defects (e.g., faulty components, assembly errors).
- Hardware failures not caused by external damage or misuse.
- Premature failure of structural parts under normal operating conditions.
- Display or screen defects not caused by physical impact (e.g., dead pixels
  exceeding the manufacturer's threshold).

### 1.2 What Is Not Covered

The warranty does **not** cover:

| Exclusion                              | Detail                                                          |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Physical damage                       | Cracks, dents, broken screens caused by drops or impact        |
| Liquid damage                         | Any exposure to water, moisture, or other liquids               |
| Normal wear and tear                  | Battery capacity loss, cosmetic scratches, faded finishes       |
| Unauthorised modifications            | Hardware or software modifications not approved by NovaBuy      |
| Third-party repairs                   | Damage caused by repairs performed outside NovaBuy service      |
| Consumable parts                      | Batteries, ink cartridges, filters expected to deplete over time|
| Environmental damage                  | Damage from power surges, extreme temperatures, or corrosion    |
| Cosmetic damage only                  | Scratches or marks that do not affect product functionality     |

### 1.3 Warranty Duration by Category

| Product Category            | Warranty Duration |
|-----------------------------|-------------------|
| Electronics & Gadgets       | 2 years           |
| Home Appliances             | 2 years           |
| Computing Accessories       | 2 years           |
| NovaBuy Audio Products      | 2 years           |
| Furniture & Home Furnishings| 1 year            |
| Clothing & Footwear         | 90 days (defects) |

## 2. Third-Party Product Warranties

Products sold on NovaBuy but manufactured by third-party brands are covered
by the **manufacturer's own warranty**, not the NovaBuy warranty.

- Manufacturer warranty terms vary by brand and product category, typically
  ranging from 1 to 3 years.
- Warranty terms for third-party products are listed on the product page under
  **Warranty & Support**.
- To make a warranty claim on a third-party product, you may either:
  - Contact the manufacturer directly using the details included with the product, or
  - Contact NovaBuy support, and we will facilitate the claim on your behalf
    where we have a direct relationship with the manufacturer.

---

## 3. How to Make a Warranty Claim

### 3.1 Before You Claim

Check the following before submitting a claim:

1. Confirm your product is within its warranty period (calculated from the
   delivery date on your order confirmation email).
2. Check that the fault is not excluded under Section 1.2.
3. Review our **FAQ** and **product support page** — many common issues can be
   resolved without a return.

### 3.2 Submitting a Claim

To submit a warranty claim, contact our support team through any channel listed
in Section 7. Please provide:

- Your **order number** (format: NB-XXXXXXX).
- The **product name and model number** (found on the product label or packaging).
- A clear **description of the fault**.
- **Photographs or a short video** demonstrating the defect.

### 3.3 Assessment Process

Once your claim is submitted:

1. Our warranty team will review your evidence within **3 business days**.
2. We may ask follow-up questions or request additional photos.
3. If the fault is confirmed as covered, we will confirm your resolution option.
4. If the claim is not covered, we will explain the reason and offer alternative
   options (e.g., paid repair, out-of-warranty service).

### 3.4 Resolution Options

Depending on availability and the nature of the defect, we will offer one of
the following:

| Resolution          | Details                                                               |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Replacement         | A new or certified-refurbished unit dispatched at no cost             |
| Repair              | Item collected, repaired by NovaBuy service, and returned             |
| Full Refund         | Refund to original payment method (see Refund Policy)                 |
| Partial Refund      | If you choose to keep a partially functional item                     |

NovaBuy reserves the right to choose the most appropriate resolution. We will
always prioritise replacement or repair over refund for items within warranty
where stock is available.

### 3.5 Warranty Return Shipping

If we require the faulty item to be returned for inspection or repair:

- NovaBuy will provide a **prepaid return label** at no cost to you.
- Please package the item carefully to prevent transit damage. Damage caused
  by inadequate packaging during return transit is not covered.
- Once received, we will aim to complete inspection and dispatch a replacement
  or repaired unit within **7 business days**.

## 4. Proof of Purchase

A valid proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims. Accepted proof
of purchase includes:

- Your NovaBuy order confirmation email.
- Your NovaBuy account order history (accessible after login).
- A physical receipt issued by NovaBuy at point of sale.

Warranty claims cannot be processed without proof of purchase. If you received
the item as a gift, ask the original purchaser for their order number.

---

## 5. Warranty Transfers

NovaBuy own-brand warranties are linked to the original order and are
**non-transferable**. If you resell a NovaBuy product, the warranty does not
transfer to the new owner. Third-party manufacturer warranties may differ —
refer to the manufacturer's terms.

---

## 6. Out-of-Warranty Repairs

If your product is outside its warranty period, or the fault is not covered
under warranty, NovaBuy offers a **paid out-of-warranty repair service** for
selected product categories.

- To request a repair quote, contact support with your product details and
  a description of the fault.
- A quote will be provided within 3 business days.
- You are under no obligation to proceed after receiving a quote.
- Repair costs vary depending on product type and fault severity.
- Repaired items are covered by a **90-day repair warranty** on the work performed.

NovaBuy does not offer out-of-warranty repairs for all product categories.
If a repair is not possible, we will advise on disposal or recycling options.

---

## 7. Consumer Rights

This warranty policy does not affect your statutory rights under the **Consumer
Rights Act 2015** (UK). Under that Act, goods must be of satisfactory quality,
fit for purpose, and as described. If a product develops a fault within 30 days
of delivery, you are entitled to a full refund under our Return & Refund Policy.
Between 30 days and 6 months, you are entitled to a repair or replacement (and
a refund if these are not possible). After 6 months, the burden of proof shifts
to the customer to demonstrate the fault existed at the time of purchase.

NovaBuy's warranty provides additional coverage beyond your statutory rights
and does not replace or diminish them.
