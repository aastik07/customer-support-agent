# Return & Refund Policy — NovaBuy

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

We want you to be completely satisfied with your NovaBuy purchase. If something
isn't right, this policy explains your options, eligibility rules, and how to
start a return or claim a refund.

---

## 1. Return Eligibility

### 1.1 Standard Return Window

You may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date** for any reason,
provided the item meets the conditions in Section 1.2.

### 1.2 Condition Requirements

To be accepted for return, an item must be:

- **Unused and undamaged** — in the same condition in which it was received.
- **In its original packaging** — including all boxes, inserts, accessories,
  manuals, and any promotional items included with the order.
- **Complete** — all components, cables, and parts must be present.

Items returned in a condition that does not meet these requirements may be:
- Refused and returned to the customer at their cost, or
- Subject to a partial refund reflecting the diminished value of the item.

### 1.3 Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned unless they are faulty or not as described:

| Category                          | Reason                                    |
|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Perishable goods                  | Health and safety                         |
| Opened software or digital codes  | Cannot be re-sold once activated          |
| Personalised or custom-made items | Made to order; cannot be resold           |
| Intimate apparel and swimwear     | Hygiene reasons (if hygiene seal removed) |
| Hazardous materials               | Legal and safety restrictions             |
| Earphones and earbuds (opened)    | Hygiene reasons                           |
| Gift cards and vouchers           | Non-refundable by their nature            |

If you believe a non-returnable item is faulty or was not as described, contact
support and we will assess your case individually.

### 1.4 Extended Returns — Holiday Period

Orders placed between **1 November and 31 December** each year are eligible for
an extended return window until **31 January** of the following year, to
accommodate gift purchases.

## 2. How to Start a Return

### 2.1 Self-Service Returns (Recommended)

1. Log in to your NovaBuy account.
2. Navigate to **My Orders** and find the relevant order.
3. Click **Return Item** and select the item(s) you wish to return.
4. Choose a return reason from the list.
5. Select your preferred return method (see Section 3).
6. Download and print the prepaid return label, or have a QR code sent to
   your email to scan at the drop-off point.
7. Pack the item securely and attach the label.
8. Drop the parcel at the carrier's approved drop-off location within **14 days**
   of initiating the return.

### 2.2 Contact Support

If you are unable to initiate a return through your account (e.g., the item
was a gift, or the order does not appear in your account), contact our support
team. We will raise the return manually and email you a return label.

### 2.3 What Happens Next

Once your return is received at our warehouse, our team will inspect the item
within **2 business days**. You will receive an email confirming:
- That the return has been accepted and a refund is being processed, or
- That the return has been rejected, with a reason and next steps.

## 3. Return Shipping

### 3.1 Free Returns

Return shipping is **free** in the following situations:

- The item arrived damaged or defective.
- You received the wrong item.
- The item was not as described on the product page.
- The item developed a fault within the warranty period.

### 3.2 Paid Returns (Change of Mind)

For all other returns (e.g., you changed your mind, ordered the wrong size,
or no longer want the item), a return shipping fee of **£3.99** is charged.
This fee is deducted automatically from your refund — you do not need to pay
it upfront.

### 3.3 Large or Heavy Items

For items classified as large or heavy (e.g., furniture, large appliances),
a collection service will be arranged instead of a drop-off return. Our team
will contact you to schedule a convenient collection slot. A collection fee
of **£9.99** applies for change-of-mind returns of large items.

### 3.4 International Returns

Customers returning items from outside the UK are responsible for the cost of
return shipping and any applicable customs charges. We recommend using a tracked
service and retaining proof of postage. NovaBuy is not responsible for returns
lost in transit.

## 4. Refunds

### 4.1 Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout. We cannot
redirect refunds to a different card or account.

| Payment Method       | Refund Timeline After Approval  |
|----------------------|---------------------------------|
| Credit / Debit Card  | 3–5 business days               |
| PayPal               | 1–3 business days               |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 3–5 business days (to linked card) |
| NovaBuy Store Credit | Instant                         |
| Bank Transfer (BACS) | 5–7 business days               |

> Timelines reflect how long NovaBuy takes to process the refund. Additional
> time may be required by your bank or payment provider before funds appear
> in your account.

### 4.2 Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued in the following cases:

- The item shows signs of use, damage, or missing components not caused by a defect.
- Only part of a bundle or multi-item order is being returned.
- A return shipping fee has been deducted (Section 3.2 or 3.3).

### 4.3 Refund Without Return

In certain cases, NovaBuy may approve a refund without requiring the item to
be physically returned. This may apply when:

- The item is unsafe or impractical to return (e.g., a heavily contaminated or
  broken item).
- The cost of return shipping exceeds the value of the item.

This is decided on a case-by-case basis by our support team.

### 4.4 Original Shipping Charges

Original shipping charges are refunded only if:

- The return is due to a NovaBuy error (wrong item sent, item damaged on arrival), or
- The entire order is returned and the return is due to a product fault.

For change-of-mind returns, the original shipping charge is not refunded.

### 4.5 Promotional Orders and Discounts

If a discount code or promotional offer was applied to your order, refunds will
reflect the amount actually paid, not the full retail price. If returning part
of a multi-item order where a bundle discount was applied, the discount will be
recalculated proportionally across remaining items.

## 5. Damaged, Defective, and Incorrect Items

### 5.1 Reporting a Problem

Please report damaged, defective, or incorrect items within **7 days of delivery**.
To do so, contact our support team with:

- Your order number (format: NB-XXXXXXX).
- A clear description of the issue.
- Photographs showing the damage, defect, or incorrect item (and the packaging
  label if applicable).

### 5.2 Resolution Options

Upon verification, we will offer one of the following resolutions at your choice:

- **Replacement** — a new item dispatched at no additional cost, with express
  shipping applied where available.
- **Full refund** — including original shipping charges.
- **Partial refund** — if the item is usable despite minor cosmetic damage and
  you choose to keep it.

We reserve the right to request the return of defective or incorrect items before
issuing a replacement or refund for high-value products.

## 6. Exchanges

NovaBuy does not currently offer direct item exchanges. If you need a different
size, colour, or variant, please:

1. Return the original item following the process in Section 2.
2. Place a new order for the item you want.

If the item you want is in limited stock, we recommend placing the new order
before initiating the return to avoid missing out.

---

## 7. Gifts

### 7.1 Returning a Gift

If you received an item as a gift and wish to return it, contact our support
team. You will need either:

- The original order number (ask the gift-giver if needed), or
- The gift receipt included with your parcel (where applicable).

### 7.2 Gift Refunds

By default, refunds are issued to the original purchaser's payment method.
If a gift receipt was included, you may choose to receive:

- **NovaBuy Store Credit** — issued to your account instantly after the return
  is approved, for the full value of the item.

We cannot issue refunds to a different payment method than the one used for
the original purchase.

---

## 8. Warranty Claims

Returns for items that develop a fault after the standard 30-day return window
may be eligible under the NovaBuy product warranty. Please refer to our
**Warranty Policy** or contact support with your order number and a description
of the fault. Warranty claims are handled separately from standard returns.
