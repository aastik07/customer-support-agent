# Refund Policy — NovaBuy

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

This policy explains when and how NovaBuy issues refunds, what to expect at
each stage, and what to do if something goes wrong. This policy works alongside
our Return & Refund Policy and Warranty Policy.

---

## 1. Refund Eligibility

You are entitled to a refund in the following situations:

| Situation                                          | Refund Type        |
|----------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Item returned within 30 days in original condition | Full or partial     |
| Order cancelled before dispatch                    | Full               |
| Item arrived damaged or defective                  | Full               |
| Wrong item received                                | Full               |
| Item lost in transit                               | Full               |
| Warranty claim approved (repair/replacement not possible) | Full        |
| Duplicate charge on your account                   | Full               |
| Unauthorised charge confirmed by our fraud team    | Full               |
| Partial order not fulfilled                        | Partial (unfulfilled portion) |

Refunds are not issued for:

- Change-of-mind returns where the item does not meet our return condition
  requirements (see Return & Refund Policy, Section 1.2).
- Items classified as non-returnable (see Return & Refund Policy, Section 1.3),
  unless faulty.
- Orders where a refund has already been issued for the same item.
- Customs duties, import taxes, or third-party fees paid by the customer.

## 3. Refund Scenarios

### 3.1 Cancellation Refunds

If you cancel an order before it is dispatched, a full refund — including any
shipping charges — is issued automatically within **1–2 business days**.

If your cancellation request is received after dispatch, we are unable to stop
the delivery. In this case, please refuse the delivery or return the item once
it arrives. The refund will be processed after the item is received back at our
warehouse (standard return refund timelines apply).

### 3.2 Return Refunds

Once a returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse (within 2
business days of carrier delivery), we will approve or reject the return.

- **Approved returns:** Refund processed within 1 business day of approval.
- **Rejected returns:** The item will be returned to the customer at their
  expense. No refund is issued.

The following deductions may apply to return refunds:

| Deduction                          | Amount  | When Applied                                |
|------------------------------------|---------|---------------------------------------------|
| Return shipping fee                | £3.99   | Change-of-mind returns (domestic)           |
| Large item collection fee          | £9.99   | Change-of-mind returns of large/heavy items |
| Diminished value deduction         | Varies  | Item returned in a used or damaged condition|

### 3.3 Faulty or Damaged Item Refunds

If your item arrived damaged or developed a fault covered under warranty,
a **full refund** is available at your request — including the original
shipping charge. No return shipping fee is deducted.

To request this refund, contact support with your order number, photos of
the damage or fault, and your preferred resolution.

### 3.4 Lost in Transit Refunds

If your order is confirmed as lost by the carrier (or has not arrived within
the timeframes stated in our Shipping Policy), you will be offered either a
full replacement or a full refund, including original shipping charges.

NovaBuy may wait for the carrier's official lost-parcel investigation to
conclude before processing a replacement or refund for orders over £100.

### 3.5 Duplicate or Unauthorised Charges

If you notice a duplicate charge or a charge you did not authorise, contact
support immediately. Our finance team will investigate within 2 business days.
If the duplicate or unauthorised charge is confirmed, the full amount is refunded
with no deductions.

We recommend also reporting an unauthorised charge to your card issuer, who
may be able to process a chargeback in parallel.

### 3.6 Partial Order Refunds

If part of a multi-item order cannot be fulfilled (e.g., an item goes out of
stock after your order is placed), we will:

1. Notify you by email.
2. Automatically refund the unfulfilled item(s) within 1–2 business days.
3. Dispatch the remaining items as normal.

If a promotional discount applied to the whole order, the refund for the
unfulfilled item will reflect the discounted price paid, not the full retail price.

## 4. Store Credit

As an alternative to a cash refund in eligible situations, you may choose to
receive **NovaBuy Store Credit**:

- Store Credit is applied instantly to your NovaBuy account.
- It can be used on any future order and does not expire.
- Store Credit is non-transferable and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
- In some scenarios (e.g., gift returns without the original payment method),
  Store Credit may be the only available refund option.

To request Store Credit instead of a cash refund, simply let our support team
know when raising your return or refund request.

## 5. Refund Status and Tracking

### 5.1 Refund Confirmation

You will receive an email confirmation as soon as a refund is approved and
submitted. This email will include:

- The refund amount.
- The payment method being refunded.
- The estimated timeframe.

### 5.2 Checking Your Refund Status

You can view the status of any pending refund in your NovaBuy account:

1. Log in and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the relevant order.
3. Click **Refund Status** to see the current stage.

Refund statuses:

| Status             | Meaning                                                    |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Pending            | Refund is queued for processing                            |
| Processing         | Refund has been submitted to your payment provider         |
| Completed          | Refund has been sent; allow time for your bank to process  |
| On Hold            | Additional review required; support will contact you       |
| Rejected           | Refund not approved; reason provided in your account       |

### 5.3 Refund Not Received

If your refund status shows **Completed** but the funds have not appeared in
your account after the expected number of business days:

1. Check with your bank or card issuer — refunds sometimes appear as a pending
   credit before being finalised.
2. If your bank confirms no refund has been received, contact NovaBuy support
   with your order number and refund confirmation email.
3. We will provide a payment reference number that you can give to your bank
   to trace the transaction.

## 6. Disputed Refunds and Chargebacks

If you are unhappy with a refund decision, please contact our support team
before raising a chargeback with your card issuer. We aim to resolve disputes
fairly and quickly.

If you raise a chargeback with your card issuer before contacting us, it may
delay the resolution and temporarily restrict your NovaBuy account while the
dispute is under review.

We take all disputed refunds seriously and will cooperate fully with any
investigation raised by your payment provider.

---

## 7. Consumer Rights

This refund policy does not affect your statutory rights. Under the **Consumer
Rights Act 2015** (UK), you are entitled to a full refund for goods that are
not of satisfactory quality, not fit for purpose, or not as described, within
30 days of delivery. The **Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013** additionally
provide a 14-day right to cancel most online purchases, with a full refund of
the product price and standard shipping costs.

NovaBuy's 30-day return policy aligns with and in most cases exceeds your
statutory rights.
