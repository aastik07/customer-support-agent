# Frequently Asked Questions — NovaBuy Customer Support

> This document is the primary knowledge source for the CustomerSupportAgent.
> It covers the most common customer questions across all support categories.

---

## Order Tracking

**Q: How do I track my order?**
Once your order has shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email
containing a tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page.
You can also log in to your NovaBuy account, go to **My Orders**, and click
**Track Shipment** next to the relevant order.

**Q: My tracking number isn't working. What should I do?**
Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear in the carrier's system
after you receive the confirmation email. If it still doesn't work after 24 hours,
please contact our support team with your order number and we'll investigate.

**Q: My order status says "In Transit" but it hasn't moved in several days.**
Occasional delays can happen due to weather, high parcel volumes, or customs
processing (for international orders). If your tracking has not updated for more
than 5 business days, please contact support and we will open an enquiry with
the carrier on your behalf.

**Q: I received the wrong item. What do I do?**
We're sorry about that. Please contact support within 7 days of delivery with:
- Your order number
- A photo of the item you received
- A photo of the packaging label

We will arrange a free return and send the correct item at no extra charge.

## Order Cancellation

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
You can cancel an order as long as it has not yet been dispatched. To cancel:
1. Log in to your NovaBuy account.
2. Go to **My Orders**.
3. Select the order and click **Cancel Order**.

If the **Cancel Order** button is greyed out, the order has already been
dispatched and can no longer be cancelled. You may instead start a return
once the item arrives.

**Q: I just placed an order and immediately want to cancel it. Is it possible?**
Yes — orders placed within the last 60 minutes can almost always be cancelled
before they enter fulfilment. Use the cancellation steps above or contact
live support immediately for the fastest response.

**Q: I cancelled my order. When will I be refunded?**
Cancellation refunds are processed within 1–2 business days. Depending on your
payment provider, the funds may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear
in your account.

## Shipping

**Q: What shipping options are available?**
NovaBuy offers the following shipping options at checkout:

| Option            | Estimated Delivery  | Cost                     |
|-------------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days   | Free on orders over £50  |
| Express Shipping  | 2–3 business days   | £4.99                    |
| Next-Day Delivery | Next business day   | £8.99                    |
| International     | 7–21 business days  | Calculated at checkout   |

Next-Day Delivery orders must be placed before 2:00 PM (GMT) on a business day.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
Yes. We ship to over 40 countries. International orders may be subject to
customs duties and import taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer.
Delivery times vary by destination and can be affected by local customs processes.

**Q: My order hasn't arrived by the estimated delivery date.**
Estimated dates are not guaranteed. If your order is more than 2 business days
past the estimated date, please contact support with your order number. We will
investigate with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, arrange a
replacement or full refund.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
Address changes can only be made before the order is dispatched. Contact support
as soon as possible and we will attempt to update the address. Once dispatched,
we cannot redirect parcels.

## Returns

**Q: What is your return policy?**
We accept returns within **30 days** of the delivery date, provided the item is:
- In its original, unused condition.
- In its original packaging with all accessories and tags included.

Items that are damaged due to misuse, or consumable products that have been
opened, are not eligible for return. See our full Return & Refund Policy for details.

**Q: How do I start a return?**
1. Log in to your NovaBuy account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Return Item**.
3. Choose the item(s) and select a return reason.
4. Print the prepaid return label (emailed to you within minutes).
5. Drop the parcel at any approved carrier location within 14 days.

**Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?**
Return shipping is **free** for faulty, damaged, or incorrectly sent items.
For change-of-mind returns, a return shipping fee of £3.99 is deducted from
your refund.

**Q: Can I return a gift?**
Yes. Contact support with the original order number (ask the gift-giver if
needed) and we can process a return. The refund will be issued to the original
payment method unless a gift receipt was included, in which case we can offer
store credit instead.

## Refunds

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
Once we receive and inspect your returned item (usually within 2 business days
of the carrier delivering it to our warehouse), we will process your refund.
Refund timelines by payment method:

| Payment Method      | Refund Timeline          |
|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Credit / Debit Card | 3–5 business days        |
| PayPal              | 1–3 business days        |
| NovaBuy Store Credit| Instant                  |
| Bank Transfer       | 5–7 business days        |

**Q: I returned an item but haven't received my refund yet.**
First, check that the return tracking shows your parcel has been delivered to
our warehouse. If it has been more than 5 business days since delivery and you
have not received a refund confirmation email, please contact support with your
return tracking number.

**Q: Can I get a refund without returning the item?**
In exceptional circumstances (e.g., the item is unsafe to return, or the
return cost exceeds the item value), we may offer a refund without requiring
a physical return. This is decided case by case by our support team.

**Q: My refund amount is less than what I paid. Why?**
Possible reasons include:
- A return shipping fee was deducted (£3.99 for change-of-mind returns).
- A partial refund was issued because the item showed signs of use or damage.
- A promotional discount applied to the original order was deducted.

If you believe the refund amount is incorrect, contact support with your
order number and refund confirmation email.

## Warranty

**Q: Do your products come with a warranty?**
Yes. All NovaBuy own-brand products include a **2-year limited warranty**
covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.
Third-party products sold on NovaBuy carry the manufacturer's warranty,
which is typically 1–2 years. Warranty terms are listed on each product page.

**Q: What does the warranty cover?**
The NovaBuy warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects (e.g., faulty components, assembly errors).
- Hardware failures under normal use conditions.

The warranty does **not** cover:
- Physical damage caused by accidental drops, liquid damage, or misuse.
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., battery capacity degradation over time).
- Damage caused by unauthorised repairs or modifications.

**Q: How do I make a warranty claim?**
1. Contact NovaBuy support with your order number and a description of the fault.
2. Our team will ask for photos or a short video of the issue.
3. If the fault is covered, we will arrange a free repair, replacement, or refund
   depending on availability and the nature of the defect.
4. You will receive a decision within 3 business days of submitting your claim.

**Q: My product is out of the standard warranty period. Can I still get help?**
We offer a paid out-of-warranty repair service for selected product categories.
Contact support for a repair quote. We will do our best to help even if your
warranty has expired.

- Relevant photos or screenshots if applicable.

- **Live Chat**: Typically under 2 minutes during business hours.
- **Email**: Within 24 hours on business days.
- **Phone**: Wait times are usually under 5 minutes during business hours.

preferred callback time.
