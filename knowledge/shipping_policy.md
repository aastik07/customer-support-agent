# Shipping Policy — NovaBuy

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

This policy applies to all orders placed on the NovaBuy website and mobile app.
By placing an order, you agree to the shipping terms described below.

---

## 1. Standard Shipping

Standard shipping is available on all eligible orders delivered within the United Kingdom.

| Condition                      | Cost   |
|-------------------------------|--------|
| Order total under £50          | £3.99  |
| Order total £50 and above      | Free   |

- **Carrier:** Royal Mail Tracked 48 or DPD, assigned automatically based on parcel size and destination.
- **Processing time:** Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM (GMT) are processed the following business day.
- **Estimated delivery:** 5–7 business days from the date of dispatch.
- Standard shipping is not available for certain remote postcodes (see Section 6 — Shipping Exceptions).

---

## 2. Express Shipping

Express shipping guarantees a faster delivery window for customers who need their order sooner.

| Option            | Cost   | Order Cutoff        |
|-------------------|--------|---------------------|
| Express (2–3 days)| £4.99  | 2:00 PM GMT         |
| Next-Day Delivery | £8.99  | 2:00 PM GMT         |

- **Carrier:** DPD or Evri Express, depending on the delivery postcode.
- **Next-Day Delivery** is available Monday to Thursday. Orders placed on Friday before 2:00 PM will arrive the following Monday. Next-Day is not available on UK public holidays.
- Express options are available for mainland UK only. Northern Ireland, Scottish Highlands, and islands may incur additional lead times of 1–2 business days even with express selection.
- Express shipping costs are non-refundable once an order has been dispatched, except in the case of a carrier fault.

## 4. Delayed Orders

NovaBuy aims to dispatch every order within the stated processing time. However, delays can occasionally occur.

### 4.1 Dispatch Delays

If your order has not been dispatched within 2 business days of placement and you have not received a dispatch notification, please contact our support team. Common causes include:

- High order volumes during promotional periods.
- A stock availability issue with one or more items in your order.
- An address verification hold triggered by our fraud prevention system.

In the case of a stock issue, we will notify you by email and offer the choice of waiting, substituting the item, or cancelling the affected item for a full refund.

### 4.2 Carrier Delays

Once your order has been dispatched, NovaBuy is reliant on the carrier for delivery. If your tracking shows no movement for:

- **3 or more consecutive business days** (standard shipping), or
- **2 or more consecutive business days** (express shipping),

please contact our support team. We will open a formal enquiry with the carrier on your behalf.

### 4.3 Lost in Transit

If the carrier confirms an item is lost, or if a delivery has not arrived within:

- **10 business days** past the estimated delivery date (domestic), or
- **30 days** past the estimated delivery date (international),

we will classify the order as lost. You will be offered either:
- A full replacement dispatched at no extra cost, or
- A full refund to your original payment method.

NovaBuy reserves the right to wait for the carrier's official lost-parcel investigation to conclude before processing a replacement or refund for high-value orders.

## 5. Order Tracking

All NovaBuy orders include tracking as standard.

### 5.1 Receiving Your Tracking Information

A tracking number and carrier link are sent to your registered email address in your **dispatch confirmation email**. You can also view your tracking number at any time by:

1. Logging in to your NovaBuy account.
2. Navigating to **My Orders**.
3. Clicking **Track Shipment** next to the relevant order.

### 5.2 Tracking Updates

Tracking information is updated by the carrier at each scan point (e.g., collected, at depot, out for delivery). NovaBuy does not control the frequency of carrier scan updates.

It is normal for tracking to show no activity for up to 24 hours after the dispatch confirmation email is sent, as parcels are often scanned in batches.

### 5.3 Tracking Statuses Explained

| Status                  | Meaning                                                              |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Label Created           | We have prepared your shipment; the carrier has not yet collected it |
| Collected               | The carrier has collected the parcel from our warehouse              |
| In Transit              | The parcel is moving through the carrier's network                   |
| At Delivery Depot       | The parcel has arrived at the depot nearest your delivery address    |
| Out for Delivery        | The parcel is on a delivery vehicle and will arrive today            |
| Delivery Attempted      | A delivery was attempted; see carrier note for next steps            |
| Delivered               | The parcel has been delivered                                        |
| Exception               | An unexpected issue has occurred; contact support if unresolved      |

### 5.4 Failed Delivery Attempts

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful (e.g., no one was home and the item could not be left safely), the carrier will:

- Leave a delivery notification card with instructions, or
- Send an email/SMS (if you opted in to carrier notifications) with redelivery options.

Most carriers allow you to reschedule delivery, redirect to a pickup point, or authorise a safe place for the parcel to be left. If the parcel is not collected or redelivered within the carrier's holding period (typically 5–7 days), it will be returned to our warehouse. We will contact you and arrange redelivery (a redelivery fee may apply) or a refund.

## 6. Shipping Exceptions

Certain locations, products, and circumstances fall outside our standard shipping terms.

### 6.1 Remote and Restricted UK Postcodes

Standard and express shipping options may be subject to extended lead times or additional surcharges for the following areas:

- Scottish Highlands and Islands (including Orkney, Shetland, and the Western Isles)
- Northern Ireland
- Isle of Man
- Channel Islands

Customers in these areas will see applicable options and any additional charges at checkout.

### 6.2 International Restrictions

We are unable to ship certain product categories internationally due to legal or carrier restrictions. These include:

- Lithium battery products above a certain capacity (restricted by IATA regulations on air freight).
- Aerosol and pressurised products.
- Products subject to country-specific import restrictions.

If an item in your cart cannot be shipped to your selected country, you will be notified at checkout and the restricted item will be removed from the order.

### 6.3 Customs and Import Duties (International Orders)

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, and handling fees levied by the destination country's customs authority. These charges are:

- **Not included** in the product price or shipping cost paid to NovaBuy.
- **The sole responsibility** of the customer.
- Collected by the carrier or customs authority before or at the point of delivery.

NovaBuy is unable to predict or control customs charges. Refusal to pay customs duties will result in the parcel being returned to us. In this case, we will refund the product cost only; original shipping charges and any return freight costs are non-refundable.

### 6.4 PO Boxes and Forwarding Addresses

We do not ship to PO box addresses or third-party freight-forwarding addresses for orders containing high-value items (over £200). Standard and low-value orders may be shipped to PO boxes at our discretion.

### 6.5 Force Majeure

NovaBuy is not liable for shipping delays caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to: natural disasters, extreme weather events, national strikes, pandemics, or governmental restrictions. In such cases, we will communicate updates to affected customers and work with our carrier partners to minimise disruption.
